Frequently asked questions

General information. For anything specific, please contact the clinic.

How can I contact the clinic?

Call the clinic on during opening hours, or use the WhatsApp and enquiry options on this page.

Where is the clinic located?

Shop No. 1, 2, Shivkrupa Bungalow, in front of Sonai English Medium School Gate, Bhekrai Nagar, Hadapsar, Pune 412308. Use the Get Directions button for Google Maps.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Sunday, 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Bring any earlier X-rays, MRI scans, prescriptions or medical reports, and a short note of when your symptoms began and what makes them better or worse.

How do I enquire about an appointment?

Call, message on WhatsApp, or fill in the enquiry form. The clinic will confirm availability with you.

When should I seek urgent medical care instead?

If back or neck pain comes with severe weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, or follows a serious injury, go to a hospital emergency department rather than waiting for a routine appointment.
